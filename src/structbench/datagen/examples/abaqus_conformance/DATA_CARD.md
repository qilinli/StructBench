## What it is

One copper-like rod striking a frictionless rigid wall: the conformance case of `docs/datagen/abaqus-conformance.md`, and the template `structbench-datagen new` copies. An example definition: it ships no data, so the parts below that need a sweep say that none was read. Rendered with `structbench-datagen card --dataset <this dir>`.

## Model and why

An axisymmetric rod of reduced-integration quadrilaterals against an analytical rigid surface, with linear hardening and frictionless contact. It is the smallest case that asks the solver for every piece of run evidence the verification instrument reads: an energy ledger with a contact term, a rigid body's reaction, fields and history on one clock.
