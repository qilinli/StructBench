## What it is

A short cylinder upset between a fixed rigid base and a dropped rigid plate whose reference node carries the drop mass as a point mass. It is the test bed of `docs/datagen/abaqus-conformance.md`, "A rigid body with a point mass". An example definition: it ships no data, so the parts below that need a sweep say that none was read. Rendered with `structbench-datagen card --dataset <this dir>`.

## Model and why

The plate is an analytical rigid surface; its reference node carries the mass and the initial velocity and is in the field output, so the force on the plate is the mass times its stored acceleration. The mesh carries a small imperfection: its quads are not rectangles, and its levels nest by index. At level 1 a case runs in half a minute.
