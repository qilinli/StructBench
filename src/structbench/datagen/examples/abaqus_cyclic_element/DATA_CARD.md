## What it is

A block of a combined-hardening material (one backstress, an isotropic part) taken through one cycle of axial strain; cases may start from a non-zero equivalent plastic strain and backstress. It is the test bed of what `docs/datagen/abaqus-conformance.md`, "Combined hardening", establishes. An example definition: it ships no data, so the parts below that need a sweep say that none was read. Rendered with `structbench-datagen card --dataset <this dir>`.

## Model and why

At level 1 a case is one element and runs in seconds, which is what makes it a test bed: the material cards, the initial state, and the backstress in the export and in the canonical file can be checked against hand values.
